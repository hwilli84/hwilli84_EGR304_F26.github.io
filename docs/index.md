## Introduction
- This datasheet is meant to document the process of taking the sensor measurements from my other teammates and using it to actuate 2 motors and a solenoid.

## Project Summary
- One motor moves in a single direction that turns a fan to cool a plant. The other motor is bi-directional and moves a shade to give a plant more or less shade. The solenoid is used to just linearly move a water valve on or off to allow water to flow through a pipe towards the plants.
- The actuators work through input received from Troy's and Carlo's board and then used to drive input output pins on 2 motors and a solenoid.
